Before you write to us

Including these details helps us resolve your issue faster.

  • Device model and platform - for example, iPhone 15, iPad Pro, or MacBook Pro
  • Operating system version
  • Clickt app version
  • Clear issue summary and what you expected to happen
  • Screenshot or short screen recording if possible
