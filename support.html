Get unstuck quickly

Try the quick fixes below, then write to us if you still need help. We support Clickt on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android.

Common issues

Export failed

Try PNG export first to verify rendering, then retry PDF, DOC, or PPT. On Android, files save to Downloads.

Open troubleshooting

Notification not arriving

Confirm device permissions and signed-in status. On Android, also check battery optimisation settings.

Check notification guide

Team actions unavailable

Verify invite acceptance and team role permissions for assignment and decision actions.

Review team permissions

Need a module walkthrough

Follow the quick-start path for Builder, Presentation, Teams, and Checklist in the User Guide.

Before you write to us

Including these details helps us resolve your issue faster.

  • Device model and platform — for example, iPhone 15, Samsung Galaxy S24, or MacBook Pro
  • Operating system version
  • Clickt app version
  • Clear issue summary and what you expected to happen
  • Screenshot or short screen recording if possible
