Exam preparation and school events need the principal's office, teachers, and administrative staff pulling in the same direction, but responsibilities live in scattered messages and spreadsheets.

Assign each responsibility directly, to the whole department, or leave it open to claim — so the principal's office always knows what's covered. Ask ClicktAI who should take it based on current workload, or what's still pending, for an instant answer.
