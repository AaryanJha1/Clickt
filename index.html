Why Teams Switch

Stop doing the repeatable work yourself

Less confusion

Problem: work gets assigned by memory, so nobody's sure who owns what - or when it's actually done.

Clickt solution: assign it to one person, the whole team, or leave it open to claim. The assignee is notified the moment it lands on them; you're notified the moment it's done.

Less follow-up

Problem: team tasks, your own errands, and daily reminders live in different apps, so you're still chasing your own to-do list.

Clickt solution: every task you're assigned flows straight into your personal checklist - or ask the Checklist Copilot to build one for anything and lay out the plan.

Faster updates

Problem: turning a spreadsheet into a report, or a report into a deck, eats the afternoon.

Clickt solution: ask Builder to find the story in your data, then ask Presentation to draft the whole deck - or refine it slide by slide with you - ready to export, preview, or open in a browser.

Can I use Clickt even if my team is small?

Yes. Clickt works for solo users, small teams, and growing teams. Start with one project and scale at your own pace.

  • Start with one project in Teams and Checklist.
  • Add Builder and Presentation when your reporting grows.
  • Keep one simple system as your team grows.
Watch the Copilot demo See small-team examples
Do I need to replace all my tools on day one?

No. Most teams start with one workflow in Clickt and expand when ready.

  • Week 1: move planning and ownership into Teams.
  • Week 2: standardize recurring steps in Checklist.
  • Later: connect Builder and Presentation for reporting.
