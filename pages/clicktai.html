A simple flow

From request to ready-to-review work.

Use the same deliberate sequence whether you are preparing a small task or an organization-wide plan.

  1. Ask

    Describe what needs to happen in everyday language.

  2. Prepare

    ClicktAI drafts a proposal in the right module.

  3. Review

    Check the proposed owners, steps, data, or story.

  4. Apply

    Approve only the work you want in your workspace.
