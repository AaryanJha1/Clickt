Teams

Put ownership where the work happens.

Teams gives people one shared place for membership, tasks, decisions, and announcements—so the next step has a visible owner and status.

Released on Apple and Android platforms

Download Clickt See the workflow

What Teams does

A practical place to coordinate people and follow-through.

Teams keeps the core coordination objects together without asking people to reconstruct the story across messages and separate trackers.

01

Manage team membership

Create teams, work with members, and keep membership requests and team context in one place.

02

Make ownership explicit

Create direct tasks for a person or share claimable work that a team member can take on.

03

Keep decisions in context

Use decision flows and announcements alongside the work they affect instead of losing them in a separate thread.

04

See work move forward

Review owners, deadlines, and task statuses so a team can focus its next conversation on what needs attention.

05

No more chasing people for updates

Tasks with a deadline get a reminder 15 minutes before it’s due, then a follow-up every 3 hours for as long as it’s still open—and the reminders stop the moment it’s marked done. Instead of messaging someone to ask “is this done yet?”, you check the team view. Less status-chasing, more actual work.

A clear four-step team rhythm.

Use the same simple sequence whether you are coordinating a project, a department, or a recurring operational handoff.

  1. 1

    Set up the team

    Create the group and establish the people who need to see its work.

  2. 2

    Put work in context

    Add a direct task, a claimable task, an announcement, or a decision request.

  3. 3

    Make the next owner clear

    Assign work or let the right teammate claim it, with an understandable deadline and status.

  4. 4

    Review the signal

    Use the team view to see progress and decide what needs a follow-up.
