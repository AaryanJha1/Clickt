Clickt for restaurants

Keep opening, service, and close-down routines consistent.

For owners, restaurant managers, kitchen, front-of-house, and operations teams coordinating a fast-moving service across one or more locations.

Download Clickt Send a project brief
The recurring coordination problem

Keep the work from one shift visible to the next.

Prep, handoff, inventory follow-up, and closing duties can be assigned verbally at the start of a shift. When service gets busy, the next shift may not know what carried over or who owns it.

The existing system Clickt does not replace

Keep the POS and inventory systems in place.

Clickt complements the point-of-sale, inventory, ordering, payroll, and accounting systems your restaurant already uses. It organizes the operational coordination around them; it does not become the record for sales or stock.

Where Clickt fits

A shift routine from setup to owner update.

Example: coordinating opening, service, and follow-up work for a busy shift.

When relevant, ClicktAI can prepare a reviewable checklist or outline. Nothing changes until a person reviews and approves it.

  1. Teams

    Give prep, service, cleaning, or supplier follow-up work a clear owner and a status the next shift can see.

  2. Checklist

    Use repeatable opening, closing, or handoff checklists so the essential routine is easier to complete consistently.

  3. Builder

    Review an approved operational view—such as completion or follow-up status—to see what needs the manager’s attention.

  4. Presentation

    Prepare a short weekly operating update from the current team view for an owner or management conversation.

Expected operational result More consistent shift follow-through

Make the routine easier to carry forward.

Clickt can help teams keep recurring work, handoffs, and management updates visible around the systems that already run sales and inventory.

What Clickt does not do. Clickt does not replace a POS, inventory platform, food-safety programme, payroll system, accounting system, or the restaurant’s operational and safety responsibilities.
