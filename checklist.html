Checklist

Turn recurring work into a list people can finish.

Checklist makes personal and team-assigned work easier to see, prioritise, and complete—without losing the practical details inside the process.

Released on Apple and Android platforms

Download Clickt See the workflow

What Checklist does

A flexible checklist for personal responsibility and shared follow-through.

Use the amount of structure the work needs—from one clear next action to a repeatable process with sub-items and a visible deadline.

01

Everything you owe, in one list

Tasks assigned to you in Teams show up here automatically, right alongside your own personal items—so there’s one list to check, not a personal list plus a separate team inbox to remember.

02

Add the right detail

Use priorities, deadlines, daily/weekly/monthly recurrence, and sub-items when a task needs more than a title.

03

Shape the working view

Filter and reorder items to focus on what is due, what matters most, and what should happen next.

04

Turn a file into a checklist

Attach a PDF, Word doc, text, or CSV file in ClicktAI and it drafts a checklist from it, so you are not retyping a document line by line. Track completion progress as you go.

05

Deadlines that follow up on their own

Any item with a deadline gets a reminder 15 minutes before it’s due, then a follow-up every 3 hours while it’s still open. Mark it done, and the reminders stop—no separate app to check, no forgetting something because it scrolled out of view.

A dependable way to run repeatable work.

Whether the list is yours or shared with a team, the workflow stays focused on a practical next step and a clear completion state.

  1. 1

    Add the work

    Start a checklist item directly, receive a team-assigned item, or bring an existing list into the workspace.

  2. 2

    Set its shape

    Add a priority, due date, recurrence, or sub-items when the work needs a reliable process.

  3. 3

    Focus the list

    Filter and reorder the work so today’s attention is on the most relevant items.

  4. 4

    Finish and review

    Complete items and use progress to see what is done, what remains, and what recurs next.
