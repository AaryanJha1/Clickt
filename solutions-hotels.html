Solutions / Hotels

Clickt for hotels

Keep service follow-up clear across every shift.

For property managers, front-desk, housekeeping, facilities, and service teams coordinating the day-to-day work around a guest stay.

Download Clickt Send a project brief

The recurring coordination problem

Keep service work visible when the shift changes.

Service recovery, facilities follow-up, housekeeping coordination, and shift routines can move verbally between people. When the shift changes, the next person may not have one shared picture of what still needs attention.

The existing system Clickt does not replace

Keep the PMS and booking systems in place.

Clickt sits beside the property-management, booking, billing, and guest-profile systems your hotel already uses. It provides a coordination layer for the team work around those systems, rather than replacing them.

Where Clickt fits

A service follow-up, clear from shift to manager update.

Example: coordinating a non-sensitive facilities or guest-service follow-up across a shift.

  1. 01

    Teams

    Give the service, housekeeping, or facilities follow-up a clear owner and status so the next shift can see the current state.

  2. 02

    Checklist

    Use repeatable opening, closing, or facilities routines to keep essential service steps consistent across shifts.

  3. 03

    Builder

    Review an approved operational completion view to identify where follow-up is still needed across the team.

  4. 04

    Presentation

    Prepare a concise manager update about operational follow-through and service work.

When relevant, ClicktAI can prepare a reviewable checklist or outline. Nothing changes until a person reviews and approves it.

A clearer shift-to-shift picture

Expected operational result

Give the next shift a visible starting point.

Clickt can help a hotel coordinate operational ownership and repeatable routines around the property-management system already in use.

What Clickt does not do

Clickt does not replace a PMS, booking engine, billing system, guest-profile system, or the hotel’s service and privacy policies.
