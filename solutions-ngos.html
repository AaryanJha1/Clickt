Clickt for NGOs

Keep programme coordination close to the work.

For programme managers, field coordinators, administrative teams, and partners organising work across locations, activities, and reporting moments.

Download Clickt Send a project brief

The recurring coordination problem

Keep a programme plan connected to its next action.

Field follow-up, programme activities, partner coordination, and reporting preparation can become scattered across group messages, documents, spreadsheets, and meetings. The plan exists, but its next actions are not always easy to see together.

The existing system Clickt does not replace

Keep donor, finance, and M&E systems in place.

Clickt complements the financial, donor-management, monitoring-and-evaluation, and record systems your organization already relies on. It helps coordinate the everyday work around them; it does not replace their source-of-truth role.

Where Clickt fits

A programme activity from field follow-up to update.

Example: preparing a recurring programme activity across a field team and central office.

  1. 01

    Teams

    Give field preparation, partner follow-up, logistics, and central-office actions a visible owner and current status.

  2. 02

    Checklist

    Use a repeatable activity checklist so the team can run the same essential preparation and follow-up steps each time.

  3. 03

    Builder

    Bring an approved programme-activity completion view into Builder to clarify progress before a review.

  4. 04

    Presentation

    Prepare a concise programme update for leadership, a partner conversation, or an internal review.

When relevant, ClicktAI can prepare a reviewable checklist or outline. Nothing changes until a person reviews and approves it.

Connected programme follow-up

Expected operational result

Keep activity, ownership, and updates in one flow.

Clickt can help teams connect field coordination, recurring activity steps, and clear progress updates without claiming to replace the programme systems they already use.

!

What Clickt does not do

Clickt does not replace financial, donor-management, monitoring-and-evaluation, beneficiary-record, or safeguarding systems, and it does not validate programme results on an organization’s behalf.
