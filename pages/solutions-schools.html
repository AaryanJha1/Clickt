Clickt for schools

Coordinate the work around school life.

For principals, academic leaders, department heads, and administrative teams coordinating the work that sits between teaching, operations, and leadership.

Download Clickt Send a project brief
The recurring coordination problem

Keep key school work from disappearing into separate threads.

Exam preparation, school events, departmental initiatives, and meeting follow-ups often move through separate messages, spreadsheets, and conversations. The work is important, but its owner and current status are not always visible.

The existing system Clickt does not replace

Keep the school-management system in place.

Clickt complements the school-management system already used for records, timetables, attendance, and other source-of-truth work. It gives the operational work around it one shared place to be assigned, followed up, and explained.

Where Clickt fits

A school event, clear from plan to update.

Example: preparing a school-wide examination week or event.

When relevant, ClicktAI can prepare a reviewable checklist or outline. Nothing changes until a person reviews and approves it.

  1. Teams

    Give facilities, communication, invigilation, and administrative follow-ups a clear owner and visible status.

  2. Checklist

    Turn repeated examination-week or event steps into a reusable checklist, so the team can work through the same essentials each time.

  3. Builder

    Bring a simple, non-student-specific progress view into Builder to see which preparation work is complete and what still needs attention.

  4. Presentation

    Turn the current status into a concise leadership update for the next meeting or decision point.

Expected operational result Clear ownership before key dates

A calmer path to the next important date.

Clickt can keep the people, recurring work, and status updates around an important school activity in one connected workflow—without asking the school to replace its existing records system.

What Clickt does not do. Clickt does not replace a school-management system, student-information record, learning platform, or the school’s own policies and professional judgement.
