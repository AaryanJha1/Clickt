Questions, answered.

Is Clickt free?
Yes. Free includes Teams, Checklist, Builder, Presentation and Calendar, and you connect your own AI provider key for ClicktAI. Business adds a company workspace, admin controls and Clickt-provided AI for teams.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone, iPad and Mac through the App Store. Android is in invite-only testing on Google Play, so request access first.
Can ClicktAI change my work without asking?
No. Every write action shows a preview first and waits for your approval. Nothing is applied until you say so.
Do I need my own AI account?
On Free, yes. You connect an OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or NVIDIA key, and usage is billed to your own account. On Business, your admin can assign Clickt-provided AI to the people who need it.
Does Clickt replace our existing software?
No. It is the coordination layer beside your ERP, school-management system, core banking or spreadsheets. If you need a tailored integration, Click T can build it.
